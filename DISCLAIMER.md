# Clinical disclaimer

`precision-dose` performs pharmacokinetic calculations for use by qualified clinicians.
It is a computational decision aid.

- It is **not a medical device**. It has not been cleared or approved by any regulatory
  authority (FDA, EMA, MHRA or any other), and it is not certified for clinical use.
- It must **not be the sole basis for a prescribing decision**. Every recommendation
  carries the library entry hash, the citations and the stated limitations so that the
  responsible pharmacist or physician can check it.
- **Drug library parameters are transcribed from published sources and must be validated
  by the responsible clinician before clinical use.** Entries that are not fully sourced
  ship as `status = "unresolved"` and the engine refuses to dose them.
- The software is provided under the MIT licence, **without warranty of any kind**,
  including fitness for a particular clinical purpose.

If you are a patient: this is developer tooling. Talk to your prescriber.
